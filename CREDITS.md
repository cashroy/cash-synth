### Currency rules (18 U.S.C. 504)

Only the front is shown (one-sided); the back of our bill is the Advanced panel. It is shown
tinted and faded with parody text on top, so it can't be mistaken for a real note, and the
bill is drawn about 1076 points wide (a real note is 6.14 in), which is well over 150% of real
size on normal displays. Don't add a print/export of the bill at real size.
